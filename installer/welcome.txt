Welcome to Relic.

Relic is a polyphonic synthesizer. This installer can put in two things:

  - the plugin (VST3), for Ardour and other music software
  - the standalone app, which runs on its own

After installing the plugin, rescan plugins in your music software. In Ardour: Window > Plugin Manager > Scan.
